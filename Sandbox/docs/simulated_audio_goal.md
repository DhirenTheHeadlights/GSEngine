# Goal: Simulated audio — sound synthesised from physics and propagated through the scene

## Aim
Audio is the engine's weakest system. Take it from clip playback to a simulated approach: sounds of rigid contact are synthesised from the physics solver's contacts and each object's material, and every sound reaches the listener through the scene's geometry (distance, direction, occlusion, reverb). Recorded clips remain for what simulation cannot cover (voices, music), and go through the same propagation.

The work follows the eight-stage progression the owner agreed to in chat. Stage 9 (wave solver, diffraction, transmission, fluids, fracture) is not part of this goal.

- The module plays `audio_clip` assets on voices through miniaudio's high-level `ma_engine` / `ma_sound`. Requests are `play`, `stop`, `pause`, `resume`, `set_volume`, `set_master_volume`.

## Pass criteria
Every measured criterion is checked from WAV files and log lines of scenario runs by a new `Tools/audio_probe.py`. "Holds" means in 3 consecutive runs unless stated.

1. **Offline render exists and is deterministic.** A scenario run can write the mixed output to a WAV rendered from the fixed-step clock (a fixed number of samples per step, no device involved), including in headless runs. The run logs the file path and a hash of the samples. Two runs of the same CPU-solver scenario give the same hash.
2. **The engine owns the sample stream, safely.** Clip voices and procedural voices mix in engine code; miniaudio is only the device. During `audio_stress` (at least 128 simultaneous voices) the real-time callback logs zero underruns and zero allocations on the audio thread, and mix time p95 is at most 25% of the block period.
3. **Positioned sources behave physically** (`audio_spatial_probe`, a tone source at scripted positions):
   - level falls 6 dB ± 0.5 dB per doubling of distance;
   - arrival delay equals distance divided by the speed of sound, within 1 ms;
   - a source moving at a scripted speed shows the predicted Doppler shift within 1%;
   - a source at 90° to one side is louder in that ear and arrives there first, with an interaural delay between 0.5 and 0.8 ms in binaural mode.
4. **Occlusion** (`audio_occlusion_probe`, a wall between source and listener, then the listener walks out from behind it):
   - behind the wall the level drops at least 6 dB, and the band above 4 kHz drops at least 6 dB more than the band below 250 Hz;
   - while walking out, no 10 ms block differs from the previous one by more than 1 dB.
5. **Contact events** (`audio_contact_probe`, bodies dropped on a floor, CPU solver, then the GPU solver variant):
   - each first impact logs exactly one event, with impulse, relative speed and both bodies;
   - a resting stack logs zero events over 300 frames;
   - event-to-sound latency is at most one fixed step on the CPU solver; the GPU solver's latency is measured and recorded;
   - `physics_stress` reports the same world-state hash as a fresh run taken before the change.
6. **Modal synthesis is correct, not just present.**
   - Baked modes of a free steel bar match the analytic free-free beam frequencies within 5% for the first four bending modes.
   - A rendered impact has spectral peaks within 1% of the baked mode frequencies, and each mode's decay time within 10% of the damping model.
   - Output level rises monotonically with impact speed across five scripted drop heights.
   - At least three materials (metal, wood, glass or ceramic) are authored, and 64 simultaneously ringing objects stay inside criterion 2's budget.
7. **Continuous contact.** A box sliding down a ramp and a sphere rolling on a floor each produce sustained output whose RMS level rises monotonically with speed over three scripted speeds, and falls below −80 dBFS within 100 ms of coming to rest.
8. **Reverb follows the room** (`audio_reverb_probe`, an impulse in two closed box rooms with different absorption, then in the open):
   - measured decay time (RT60) is within 20% of the Eyring prediction for each room;
   - in the open, energy 200 ms after the direct sound is at least 60 dB down;
   - with a stationary listener and source, logged reverb parameters vary by less than 5% between frames.
9. **Nothing else regresses.** The build through `gse_build` is clean. `physics_stress`, `dominoes_bench` and `cloud_showcase` pass, with frame p50 inside the existing regression threshold. `Web/Audio.cpp` matches the module interface.
10. **Owner sign-off.** The owner listens to the probe WAVs and to a live Sandbox session and approves the result as believable. This is an owner criterion, and it is asked for at the end of stages 4, 7 and 8, not only at the end.

## Steering order
1. **Sample stream and instrument.** Engine-owned mixer with clip and procedural voices; the offline WAV sink; `Tools/audio_probe.py`; `audio_stress`. Criteria 1 and 2. Nothing later can be measured without this.
2. **Listener and positioned sources.** Attenuation, panning, per-source delay line (Doppler), air absorption. Criterion 3 except the binaural line.
3. **Binaural output.** Escalate once for the dataset, then finish criterion 3.
4. **Occlusion.** Collider ray query, several rays per source, smoothed. Criterion 4. Ask for the owner's first listen.
5. **Contact events with clips.** Event export, thresholds, voice limiting, clips triggered from events. Criterion 5.
6. **Modal synthesis.** Offline mode bake, resonator bank, three materials. Criterion 6. Start with boxes and bars, where the answer can be checked analytically.
7. **Sliding and rolling.** Criterion 7. Ask for the owner's second listen.
8. **Ray-traced reverb.** Energy decay from collider rays into a feedback delay network, plus early reflections. Criterion 8.
9. Run the regression set and get the final sign-off.

## Assumptions
- **Scope is stages 1–8.** The owner said "make this into a goal" about the whole progression; stage 9 was presented as optional and is left out. If the owner wants a shorter goal, criteria 5–8 are the ones to cut.
- **Rays go against physics colliders, not the render acceleration structure.** That keeps audio working headless and deterministic, which criterion 1 depends on. The cost is that geometry without a collider does not block or reflect sound. If that proves audible in the Sandbox scenes, steering escalates.
- **The thresholds were chosen from textbook behaviour, without a measured baseline.** If a threshold is unreachable for a reason unrelated to quality, steering escalates instead of changing the number.
- **Determinism is only claimed on the CPU solver.** The GPU solver is known to be non-deterministic, so its probe runs are judged on the measured properties, not on a hash.
- **The audio system must run headless for the offline sink.** That means relaxing its `presents` requirement or splitting device output from mixing. Which one is settled in stage 1.
- **Not determined in scoping:** the fixed-step rate and therefore the samples per step; how late GPU-solver contacts arrive after readback; whether recorded video clips can carry an audio track (if not, sign-off uses WAVs and a live session); how mode shapes are computed for arbitrary meshes without a volumetric mesh. The last one may limit stage 6 to simple shapes, and steering records where the limit falls.
- No other chat is working in the audio or physics sources as of scoping (the two active chats are on enum formatting and settings).
